\section{Conclusion}

We presented a full-split protocol for measuring state-level repairability in diffusion reasoning trajectories. The protocol separates three questions that are often conflated: whether a failed trajectory contains a recoverable state, where that state occurs, and whether it can be selected without oracle probe outcomes. The resulting evidence is reported jointly with negative repair, cost, uncertainty, selector baselines, dataset transfer, and backbone transfer. This framing supports a scoped conclusion about measurable intermediate-state recoverability under an explicit repair probe, while leaving broader claims about self-correction or universal diffusion behavior for future work.
