\section{Analysis Plan}

\paragraph{Existence and temporal extent.}
RQ1 requires independently confirmed recovery on sampled failed reference
trajectories. The never-correct repairable rate additionally requires the
observed candidate to remain incorrect on the measured checkpoint grid.
Temporal reporting comprises $q_C(t)$, $q_R(t)$, $\Delta_R(t)$,
$T_{\mathrm{last}}^R$, and a clearly defined repairability-survival summary.
Confirmation outcomes can fluctuate over time; a survival summary must not
silently impose monotonic recovery.

\paragraph{Mechanism.}
RQ2 compares $q_R$ with matched $q_C$, reports modified positions and NFE, and
retains null, negative, and inconsistent differences. Native replay fidelity
alone does not measure stochastic continuation recoverability. Fresh sampling
must be actually decoded; an analytic pass@$k$ approximation cannot substitute
for this control.

\paragraph{Localization and transfer.}
RQ3 evaluates the frozen observable-state selector prospectively, counting harm
to successful trajectories and coverage. A selector tie or negative value is
retained. RQ4 uses the declared task and backbone matrix, with per-run
uncertainty and evidence status. Decoder-regime differences remain distinct
from cross-backbone generalization. Interpretation is deferred until the
corresponding reference evidence is sealed and reviewed by the authors.
