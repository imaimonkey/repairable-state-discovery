\section{State-Level Repairability Protocol}

\subsection{Sampled trajectory and reference validation}
The primary unit is one sampled reference trajectory per item. A trajectory is
failed if its final answer is incorrect under the frozen task evaluator.
The primary denominator comprises sampled trajectories whose final answer is
incorrect, rather than items for which all eight measurement trajectories fail.
Reference trajectory accuracy is pass@1 on this one-trajectory-per-item bank.

The decoder recipe fixes the source revision, model and tokenizer revisions,
prompt, sampling settings, schedule, and evaluator. Source-native smoke and recipe fidelity (R0) precedes evaluator bridge validation (R1) and instrumentation
equivalence (R2). R2 requires exact final token sequence, final answer,
correctness, schedule, and model-evaluation-count agreement between instrumented
and uninstrumented decoding on fixed items and seeds. Snapshot collection must
not consume additional randomness. The validated instrumented implementation
creates the trajectory bank reused by the repair experiment.

\subsection{Continuation, intervention, and independent confirmation}
Let $x_{i,t}$ be a snapshot and $o_{i,t}$ its observed candidate correctness.
For continuation operator $C$ and repair operator $R$, define
\[
q_C(x_{i,t})=\Pr[\operatorname{Eval}(C(x_{i,t};\xi))=1],\qquad
q_R(x_{i,t})=\Pr[\operatorname{Eval}(R(x_{i,t};\xi))=1],
\]
and $\Delta_R(x_{i,t})=q_R(x_{i,t})-q_C(x_{i,t})$.
Exact native continuation is a fidelity control. The estimator of $q_C$ uses
matched continuation with resampled future randomness where the reference
decoder is stochastic. The official LLaDA temperature-zero recipe is
deterministic; repeated seeds do not supply independent Bernoulli information.
The canonical repair remasks low-confidence eligible committed positions within
the active decoder phase. Matched stochastic continuation and repair share the
declared future-noise group for paired comparisons.

Localization uses $B_{\mathrm{loc}}=4$ branches. Independent confirmation uses
$B_{\mathrm{eval}}=8$ branches and canonical threshold
$\tau_{\mathrm{confirm}}=0.25$, with sensitivity thresholds $0.125$ and $0.5$.
The finite-sample maximum used for localization is not reported as an
independently confirmed oracle estimate. The seed registry identifies stage,
item, trajectory, checkpoint, branch, and intentional pairing; unintended reuse
between localization, confirmation, fresh sampling, and independent controls
must be rejected before submission of a run.

\subsection{Temporal and prospective quantities}
Snapshots correspond to the nearest valid states on normalized refinement
progress $\{0.125,0.250,0.375,0.500,0.625,0.750,0.875\}$, with final-endpoint
metadata recorded separately. Absolute step numbers need not agree between
decoders. The core procedure localizes with four branches per grid state and confirms
only the highest-localization-$q_R$ candidate, breaking ties toward earlier
progress. The temporal subset receives independent confirmation over the whole
grid. Completed LLaDA blocks are not reopened: grid targets at block boundaries
map to the nearest open-block snapshot, with ties toward the earlier step. $T_{\mathrm{last}}^R$ is the last confirmed repairable checkpoint on this
grid, conditional on the operator, branch budget, and threshold; it is not a
proof of absolute irrecoverability. No monotonicity constraint is imposed.

The frozen state-value estimator is evaluated out of fold with grouping by item.
A prospective policy selects a checkpoint on the single base trajectory using
observable signals, without conditioning deployment on its final correctness.
Prospective accuracy change includes degradation of originally successful
trajectories. Retrospective recovery and prospective policy value are reported
separately. Number of function evaluations (NFE), coverage, and harm accompany
recovery measures.
