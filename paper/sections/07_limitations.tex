\section{Limitations}

Repairability is conditional on a decoder, intervention, checkpoint grid,
branch budget, and evaluator. Failure to recover within this design does not
establish that a state is irrecoverable under every intervention.

The reference design prioritizes two mathematical tasks and two backbones.
Deep probing uses deterministic subsets of failed trajectories, whereas the
baseline uses the full one-trajectory-per-item bank. Their denominators and
uncertainty must remain explicit. Legacy measurement-decoder breadth evidence
does not fill a missing reference anchor.

The learned selector relies on probe supervision during training even when its
inference signals are observable. Grouped out-of-fold evaluation and
successful-trajectory harm are necessary for interpreting prospective value.
Reference numerical results, temporal title support, and broader empirical
conclusions are not yet established in this working draft.
