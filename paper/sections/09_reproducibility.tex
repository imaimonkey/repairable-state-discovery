\section{Reproducibility Appendix}

The machine-readable source of final results is `results/full_paper_manifest.json`. The final suite is launched with:

\begin{verbatim}
PROTOCOL_NODE=ubuntu AGGREGATE_NODE=ubuntu MAX_PARALLEL_GPUS=4 \\
HF_HOME=/data/kimhj/.cache/huggingface \\
RFBA_ROOT=/home/kimhj/Rethinking-Fixed-Block-Assumptions-in-Diffusion-Language-Model-Decoding \\
bash scripts/full_paper_pipeline.sh submit
bash scripts/full_paper_pipeline.sh finalize
\end{verbatim}

The run contract records model profiles and checkpoints, dataset paths and splits, eight trajectories per item, seeds, 64-step decoding, checkpoint schedule, repair temperatures and remasking fractions, predictor features, grouped item split, negative-repair definition, bootstrap procedure, and the branch-cost definition. Independent run-level Slurm jobs request one GPU each; protocol reports and all aggregate/analysis artifacts are produced by CPU jobs after their dependencies complete. Heavy model weights, caches, trajectories, and logs are intentionally excluded from the repository.

Qualitative examples are generated by the repository's deterministic selection script and include early recovery, late failure, and negative repair categories. The historical 200-item outputs remain available for development diagnostics but are not used as the canonical full-paper result source.
