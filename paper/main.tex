\begin{abstract}
Diffusion language models expose intermediate refinement states, but a failed final trajectory does not reveal whether an earlier state remained recoverable. We study this question as a measurement problem. We collect multi-trajectory diffusion reasoning traces, snapshot intermediate states, restart from snapshots with a local repair probe, and quantify both oracle recoverability and non-oracle selection. The evaluation treats repair gain, negative repair, selector comparisons, uncertainty, and repair cost as a joint object. The full-split results in this manuscript are generated from the canonical benchmark manifest; the paper deliberately avoids interpreting autoregressive references as a raw diffusion-versus-autoregressive claim.
\end{abstract}
